Programmes

Primary to junior secondary

Primary 1 through Secondary 3, working from the school's own pace. Students finish homework with a tutor present, then revise the day's key points. Before exams, we focus on the weakest topics.

01

Homework follow-up

Each piece of homework is worked through, then checked by a tutor — so it is not just handed in, it is done correctly.

02

Revision & dictation

We work to the school's dictation and quiz schedule, prepping each one before it arrives.

03

Exam preparation

Practice papers, marked and explained question by question, plus work on timing under pressure.
